How it works
  1. Paste a link or text above and press Make my pack.
  2. Your browser downloads a single .html file (usually under 150 KB).
  3. Open it on any phone, tablet, or laptop - it works fully offline.
  4. Read in short parts, have it read aloud, switch on the dyslexia-friendly font, check the glossary, then quiz yourself.
